HOW TO REPLACE YOUR PROFILE PICTURE:
=====================================
1. Place your professional photograph in this folder:
   c:\Users\Zaid\Desktop\Personal Portfolio\assets\images\profile\

2. Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp

3. Name your file either:
   - profile.jpg
   - profile.png
   - profile.webp

4. If you name it "profile.jpg" or "profile.png", update the <img> src in `index.html`:
   Change:
     src="assets/images/profile/profile-placeholder.svg"
   To:
     src="assets/images/profile/profile.jpg"

5. A square or portrait aspect ratio (approx 600x700px or 800x800px) will look best!
