HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL EMBROIDERY WORK:
1. Save high-resolution photos or Wilcom 3D preview exports (PNG or JPG).
2. Save them into this folder: `assets/images/digitizing/`
3. In `index.html`, find the section with id `digitizing` and update the `<img src="...">` path to your new photo file.
4. The lightbox modal will automatically open and zoom your new real high-resolution photograph!
